Our Philosophy

Fire is the Only Ingredient We Never Substitute

Every cut at Ember & Oak is dry-aged in our on-site cellar, then finished over a live oak and applewood fire. It's a slower, more deliberate way to cook a steak — and it's the only way we know how.

  • Dry-aged 38+ days in our climate-controlled cellar
  • Seared over an open live-fire hearth
  • Produce sourced from Washington & Oregon farms
